Care should fit real life

Make the practical questions part of the conversation.

A convenient format is useful only when it also fits your privacy, comfort, and care needs. Ask about what is available rather than assuming one setting works for everyone.

Can I meet at home or in person?

Both formats are part of Doppler’s care approach. Actual options depend on where you are, the clinician’s licensure and availability, and the type of support you need. Confirm the details before scheduling.

What should I ask about cost?

Ask about the clinician’s network status, your deductible, copay or coinsurance, any self-pay rate, and whether telehealth is covered. Coverage is specific to your plan and provider; it should be confirmed before care.

How does virtual-care privacy work?

Ask which platform will be used, what privacy practices apply, where you can join privately, and what happens if the connection drops. Use the patient intake form for care-related information rather than ordinary email.
