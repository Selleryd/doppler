Website privacy

A note about this website.

This page describes the behavior of this static website. It is not a clinical Notice of Privacy Practices or a statement about every external service.

Contact the team

Interactive tools stay in your tab.

The mini-quiz, reflection space, breathing exercise, and timers run locally in your browser. This site’s code does not send your tool entries to a server, save them to local storage, or attach them to external form links. Leaving the page clears in-memory writing and selections.

External forms are separate.

The questionnaire, patient intake, and clinician application open external Jotform pages. Information entered there is handled by those services and the form operator, under their own settings and notices. Review those before submitting sensitive information.

Hosting and your browser.

This package does not include advertising pixels, analytics scripts, tracking cookies, or third-party font requests. A hosting provider may still process ordinary connection information, such as IP addresses and request logs. Your browser, sync settings, or extensions may also process information independently of this site.

Clinical records and sensitive information.

For questions about clinical records, privacy rights, or the practice’s formal privacy notice, contact the Doppler team. Do not send sensitive health information through ordinary email. Use an appropriate intake or clinical communication channel.
