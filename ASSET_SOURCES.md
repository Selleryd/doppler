# Visual asset provenance

Every image shipped with this redesign is original to the Doppler Wellness project. The site contains no stock photography or stock video.

## Living-world scenes

The following editorial scenes were created specifically for the site with OpenAI ImageGen, art-directed around Doppler’s yellow, deep-navy, warm-ivory, coral, and botanical palette, then optimized locally as responsive WebP pairs:

- `scene-hero.webp` — a people-free meditation garden used in mindfulness editorial content
- `scene-arrive.webp` — entering a calm botanical threshold
- `scene-notice.webp` — an eyes-open pause beside still water
- `scene-understand.webp` — a therapist and client in a plant-filled room
- `scene-carry.webp` — moving through a courtyard into morning light
- `scene-in-person.webp` — an in-person session with a woman therapist and adult man
- `scene-virtual.webp` — an at-home tablet session with an adult man and woman therapist

The at-home and in-person modes are intentionally shown as two still editorial scenes. No motion footage is shipped.

## Older-adult care revision

- `older-adult-care.webp` — original AI-generated editorial photograph of an older adult and a therapist in a warm sunlit lounge. This is illustrative brand imagery, not a photograph of an actual Doppler patient or clinician. 1536 × 1024, converted to WebP for site use.

## V38 matching conversation

- `matching-conversation-v38.webp` and `matching-conversation-v38-sm.webp` — original image generated with the built-in image generation tool for the matching editorial section. Fictional older woman and woman therapist in a warm cream and lilac lounge, with natural eye contact, buttery afternoon light, plaster, linen and travertine materials. No actual Doppler patient or clinician is depicted. Main image: 1122 × 1402; responsive companion: 640 × 800.
- Prompt direction: cinematic painterly editorial realism; exactly two people seated in comfortable conversation; older woman around 70 in lavender and navy, clinician in her late forties in ivory; quiet arched-window architecture and soft yellow sunlight; natural anatomy, visible faces, restrained warm expressions; portrait 4:5 composition; no text, logos, diagrams or prominent trees.
