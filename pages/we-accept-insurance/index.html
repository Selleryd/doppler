Coverage should be a question you can ask clearly.

Doppler may work with Medicare and some commercial PPO plans. Participation and your specific benefits must still be confirmed.

Coverage preparation

Bring the basics. Keep private details private.

Doppler or your insurer may ask for a few details when checking benefits. You do not need to enter any of them on this page.

PlanYour insurance plan name

Use the exact name printed on your card.

Member detailsYour card, kept with you

Share identifying information only through the contact path the team designates.

PreferenceAt home or in person

Ask whether format changes your benefits or available clinicians.

Questions worth asking
Network

Is the therapist in network?

Network participation can differ by provider and plan.

Deductible

Do I have a deductible?

Ask how much has been met and what applies to outpatient therapy.

Cost

What is my copay?

Confirm the amount for each visit and whether telehealth differs.

Approval

Is authorization required?

Some plans ask for approval before or during care.
