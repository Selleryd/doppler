At Doppler Wellness, we are dedicated to providing high-quality mental health care, and we’re always looking for passionate therapists to join our growing team.

Connect with our team A little more about life at Doppler
Meaningful work, at every stage

Bring your experience.
Make a real impact.

Our mission is to offer meaningful support to individuals navigating challenges such as grief, anxiety, depression, chronic illness, life transitions, and more.

We are looking for compassionate clinicians who are eager to make a real impact in the lives of their clients. While we serve clients of all ages, we specialize in supporting older adults, who often face unique challenges like cognitive decline, caregiving stress, depression, and grief.

However, our practice remains welcoming to clients from all walks of life, ensuring that everyone receives the care and attention they deserve.

Benefits of working with Doppler

More room for
the work you do best.

A clinician-first practice with thoughtful referrals and comprehensive support.

01

Thoughtfully Matched Referrals

We connect you with clients whose needs align with your expertise, enabling you to focus on delivering the exceptional care you do best.

02

Flexible, Clinician-First Model

Enjoy the freedom to choose whether you see clients in person, virtually, or a mix of both—whatever works best for you and your clients.

03

Comprehensive Administrative Support

We take care of all onboarding and administrative tasks, so you can concentrate on what matters most: client care.

04

Collaborative, Team-Based Environment

We foster a culture of collaboration and shared insights to ensure the best outcomes for both our clients and our clinicians.

05

Professional Flexibility and Autonomy

You’ll have complete control over your caseload, choosing clients that align with your strengths and passions. Plus, you can create a schedule that fits your personal and professional needs.

06

Personalized Client Care

You’ll be able to offer tailored, meaningful support to clients, whether you're helping someone navigate grief, manage stress, or support older adults through life transitions.

07

Telehealth and In-Person Options

With the ability to offer both in-person and telehealth sessions, you can provide the most flexible, client-centered care in a way that suits both your lifestyle and the needs of your clients.

08

Continued Education & CEU Support

At Doppler Wellness, we believe in lifelong learning and professional growth. We actively support our clinicians in maintaining licensure and expanding their skill sets by offering access to continuing education opportunities. Team members receive:

  • Reimbursement for approved CEU courses and trainings.
  • Support in identifying relevant programs aligned with their clinical interests.
  • Access to internal workshops and knowledge-sharing opportunities.
  • Flexible scheduling to accommodate licensure and educational requirements.
