At-home therapy

A real session, from a place that already feels like yours.

Meet with a psychologist or licensed therapist by video when available. No commute, less friction, and space to arrive exactly as your day has left you.

Start your match
Care that fits into life

Your room. Your rhythm. Your time.

At-home therapy can make it easier to protect time for care, especially when travel, work, caregiving, health, or a full schedule would otherwise get in the way.

Arrive

Begin without the commute.

Move from your day into a focused conversation without adding travel time.

Settle

Choose a private space.

Close a door, use headphones if helpful, and settle somewhere you can speak comfortably.

Connect

Keep the relationship human.

Eye contact, quiet, questions, and careful listening still belong in the room—even when the room is digital.

Practice

Use what surrounds you.

Grounding and mindfulness tools can connect directly to the space and routines around you.

Privacy practicesAsk before you connect
Privacy deserves plain language

Understand privacy before you connect.

Before your first virtual visit, ask which telehealth platform is used, how privacy is handled, and what limits apply. Care-related information belongs in designated intake and visit workflows, not ordinary email.

  • Ask how the visit link worksKnow which platform you will use and how to enter your session.
  • Use a personal device and private networkAvoid public Wi-Fi when possible and keep your device up to date.
  • Choose a space you can controlHeadphones and a closed door can help when privacy at home is limited.
